Hello, I'm Mohsin,
an early-career researcher.

I study how cancer cells that escape the original tumor colonize healthy tissues elsewhere in the body. This matters because most cancer deaths result from metastasis rather than the original tumor. In my postdoctoral work, I ask which metabolic programs enable these disseminated cells to take hold despite harsh conditions such as low oxygen (hypoxia) and altered nutrient supply. In particular, I am examining whether these conditions alter communication between mitochondria and the nucleus—and whether that altered communication helps cancer cells survive and grow.

My research journey began during my Ph.D. at the University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences, where I investigated how DNA damage and excess mitochondrial reactive oxygen species in bone-forming cells contribute to bone loss.
